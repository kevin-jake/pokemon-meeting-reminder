Pokémon Meeting Reminder bridge

Passes the meetings that Outlook on the web and Teams load in this browser, and the meeting toasts they raise, to the tray app on this PC. Nothing leaves your computer.

1. Pair with the app

In the tray menu choose Outlook / Teams (browser) ▸ Copy pairing code and paste it here.

2. Keep Outlook open

Keep an Outlook on the web tab (or the Outlook PWA) open, and open its calendar once so the week is loaded. Teams works the same way. Meetings are sent to the app every minute.
