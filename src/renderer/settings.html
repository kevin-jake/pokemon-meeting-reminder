Reminders

Choose when a Pokémon runs across your screen to warn you about a meeting.

Timing

How long before a meeting you get the first heads-up. You always get a second reminder when the meeting starts.

Also show a regular Windows notification with the meeting title, alongside the Pokémon.

Skipped meetings

Meetings with these titles never trigger a reminder. Put one title on each line. Upper or lower case doesn't matter. Use * as a wildcard: Lunch* skips every meeting whose title starts with "Lunch".

Calendars

Connect the calendars that hold your meetings. The app checks them in the background and reminds you before each meeting.

Connected calendars

For each calendar, choose the browser profile that opens its meeting links. Pick the profile that's signed in to that account. "Default profile" uses your choice under Browser & links.

Add a calendar

Google Calendar

Sign in with Google to give the app read-only access to your events. Choose the browser to sign in with.

Calendar link (iCal)

Works with Outlook, Google, iCloud and others. Copy your calendar's secret iCal (.ics) address, then click Add. The app reads the link from your clipboard.

Outlook & Teams

Get reminders for work meetings without connecting your account. A browser extension reads them from Outlook on the web and Teams. Nothing leaves this PC.

Status

Capture

Get reminders for meetings you open in Outlook on the web or Teams in Edge or Chrome.

Outlook or Teams sometimes shows an alert for a meeting the extension hasn't captured. Turn this on to play a Pokémon reminder for those alerts too.

The browser profile used to join these meetings. This is usually the profile where you use Outlook and Teams.

Set up the extension

  1. Install the extension

    Go to edge://extensions or chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode. Click Load unpacked, then pick the folder opened by this button.

  2. Pair it with this app

    Copy the pairing code, paste it on the extension's options page, and click Save and test.

  3. Open your calendar

    Reload any open Outlook or Teams tabs, then open the Outlook calendar once. The status above shows how many meetings were captured.

Troubleshooting

Forget captured meetings

Clear every meeting the extension has sent. Use this if you get reminders for meetings that were cancelled or deleted. They come back the next time you open your calendar.
